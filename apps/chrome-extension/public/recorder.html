What can I record?

Choose a Chrome tab with “Share tab audio” for tab sound. Screen/window audio depends on your operating system and Chrome. Microphone and webcam require separate consent. Protected content may appear black.

Keep this tab open. Closing it, reloading it, removing the extension, or clearing browser data can interrupt or delete local recordings. Download important videos. Video composition runs in a dedicated worker. Test a short exported clip while switching tabs to verify your Chrome/device setup.

Open the toolbar popup for quick recording and optional browser overlays. Chrome internal pages and the Web Store do not allow page overlays. On shared pages, the browser overlay can appear in the saved video; hide it when needed. Automatic AI person cutout, cloud sharing links, and native overlays over every app are not included.
